Eclipse OS in your browser

The real kernel, not a video or a mock-up: Eclipse for riscv64 booting inside QEMU compiled to WebAssembly. It all runs on your machine.

Booting takes from half a minute to a couple of minutes, depending on your machine. Whatever you do inside is lost on reload.

Try a few commands

This is the console of the minimal variant, with busybox and Eclipse's own tools.

uname -a
cat /etc/os-release
ps
hunterctl status | head -4
top

What is behind it

The Eclipse kernel and its eclipse-init, built for riscv64 and booted with OpenSBI on QEMU's virt machine, which runs as WebAssembly with its JIT translator.

What is missing

No desktop, no network and no disk that is kept. For the full desktop, download the ISO and boot it in QEMU or on your PC.

Emulator: qemu-wasm (QEMU for the browser, GPL-2.0). Terminal: xterm.js and xterm-pty (MIT). Firmware: OpenSBI (BSD-2). The sources and how to rebuild these files are in scripts/live-web.
